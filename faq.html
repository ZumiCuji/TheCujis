Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful details as we look forward to celebrating with you.

Are children invited?

While we adore your little ones, we have chosen to celebrate our wedding with an adults-only event. We kindly ask that you make arrangements for childcare so you can relax and enjoy the evening with us. Thank you so much for understanding!

Can I bring a plus-one?

Due to limited space, we are only able to accommodate guests who have been specifically invited. Your invitation is reserved for the individual(s) whose names appear on the invitation or who received a direct electronic invitation from us. We kindly ask that you do not bring additional guests. Thank you for understanding and helping us keep our celebration intimate!

Will there be an open bar?

Yes! We will have an open bar offering complimentary beer and wine throughout the celebration. Liquor and cocktails will not be served. While drinks are on us, you are more than welcome to bring cash if you would like to tip our wonderful bartenders. We kindly ask that guests do not bring their own alcoholic beverages.

What is the dress code?

Our dress code is Formal Spring Garden Party! Think elegant springtime attire, romantic silhouettes, beautiful colors, and polished formalwear. We would love for everyone to embrace the beauty of the season while dressing for a special evening of celebration. Please see our inspiration guide below for ideas!

What is the wedding day schedule?

We cannot wait to celebrate with you! Our ceremony will begin promptly at 5:30 PM, followed by cocktail hour, dinner, and an evening of celebrating together at the reception.

  1. 5:30 PM Wedding Ceremony
  2. Following the Ceremony Cocktail Hour
  3. Following Cocktail Hour Dinner
  4. Following Dinner Reception & Celebration
